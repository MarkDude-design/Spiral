**Receipts.** Verification methods differ by world. Files 03–08 document orbit sources and their own numerical or exact checks in their headers. Files 09–11 are designed kinematic worlds, not physical gravity integrations; their headers describe their design-specific checks. Files 01–02 are original worlds. The close-encounter families in 04 and 08 show the slow precession noted in their headers. Disturbance and reset behavior varies; use each world's controls and header notes rather than assuming every reset restores a catalogued orbit.

**Adding a world:** at this repository revision the catalog has 11 entries. Add `12.html` and one corresponding entry to the `WORLDS` list in `index.html`. The viewer wraps through the catalog.
